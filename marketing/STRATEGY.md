# AMS Marketing — permanent principles

**The residential market sustains the operation. B2B drives growth. Both are strategic; neither may be neglected.**

## Two markets, two jobs
| | B2C — private clients | B2B — companies |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Families, owners, tenants moving out (Wohnungsabgabe), landlords of small properties | Retail brands, expansion managers, property/asset managers, architects, general contractors |
| Job for AMS | Cash flow, frequent jobs, team utilisation, cover fixed costs | Larger contracts, revenue growth, long-term relationships |
| Services to push | Interior painting, renovations, repairs, apartment handovers, small refurbishments | Store fit-outs & rollouts, multi-trade coordination, maintenance contracts, commercial interiors |
| Sales cycle | Days to weeks | Months |
| Creative tone | Emotion, humour, everyday life, home | Intelligent, sharp, about their business problem |

Never reject or deprioritise B2C campaigns. Never let B2C replace B2B pipeline building.

## Mandatory campaign classification
Every campaign brief starts with:
- **Audience:** B2C or B2B (plus segment).
- **Commercial objective:** one of
  1. **Immediate lead capture** — enquiries now (needs a clear call to action and contact path).
  2. **Cash generation / utilisation** — fill the schedule in a specific period (seasonal, time-bound).
  3. **Brand building** — recognition and premium perception; measured over months.
  4. **B2B contract acquisition** — conversations with decision-makers, tenders, meetings.
- **KPI:** what number tells us it worked (enquiries, booked jobs, CHF quoted, meetings, reach).
- **Capacity check:** can the team absorb the work in that period?

Creativity stays conceptual for every objective. Lead and cash campaigns add a clear offer or call to action; they do not become technical or cheap-looking.

## Seasonality (Switzerland)
| Period | Situation | Marketing focus |
|---|---|---|
| Nov – Feb | Exterior work slows, risk of idle team | **B2C interiors push**: interior painting, renovations, repairs. Plan campaigns from **September/October**, since private clients book weeks ahead. B2B: maintenance contracts, planning for spring rollouts. |
| Mar – Jun | Demand rises | B2C exterior and renovation; B2B fit-outs and rollouts for autumn store openings |
| End of Mar, end of Sep (Swiss moving dates) | Apartment handovers peak | **Wohnungsabgabe** campaigns aimed at tenants and landlords, 4–6 weeks before |
| Jul – Aug | Holidays; retail fit-outs in closed periods | B2B summer works; brand building |
| Year-round | — | B2B relationship building (architects, asset managers): slow cycle, never pause |

## Guardrails
- **Premium pricing must survive B2C promotion.** Use urgency and convenience (dates, fast start, clean work), not discounts that cheapen the brand.
- **The schedule is the truth.** Marketing intensity follows booked utilisation: when the order book for the next 6–8 weeks is thin, B2C lead campaigns go up.
- **Track every campaign** against its KPI; stop what doesn't produce enquiries or meetings within its expected window.
