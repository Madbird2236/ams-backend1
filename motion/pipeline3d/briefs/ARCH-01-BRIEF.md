# ARCH-01 «Der Schnitt» — creative brief (6 s, 9:16, no presenter, no voice)

**Audience / objective:** B2B (retail brands, asset managers, GCs). Show AMS as the
execution partner that turns a raw shell into an opening-ready store. A demo of the
3D pipeline that can become a reel opener.

**Idea (one sentence):** a single glowing section line travels through a raw retail
shell from the back wall toward the viewer. Everything the line has crossed is
finished, and everything it hasn't crossed is still Rohbau.
The line is the AMS edge (continuity with MACRO-1), scaled up from one wall to a
whole store.

**Why it is stronger than 3D-1:** in 3D-1 the transformation was a colour change on
walls. Here the transformation is *physical* and *legible in one glance*:
- boards slide onto the metal studs;
- the walls are painted;
- concrete turns into oak;
- the shelving rises out of the floor;
- the light lines switch on;
- dust disappears.

The frame always holds both states at once (before | after), so the viewer reads the
change instead of being told it.

**Beat sheet (frame-exact):**
| t (s) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–0.6 | Rohbau, cold work lamp, dust in the air, the camera is already moving (no static open) |
| 0.6–4.4 | the line runs from the back wall to the camera; behind it the store builds itself |
| ≈3.4 | the line passes through the camera: the moment of the image |
| 4.4–6.0 | reveal: finished store, warm light; the line «Vom Rohbau zur Eröffnung.» |

**Craft truth (6g):**
- drywall order is studs → boards → paint, never paint on studs;
- light lines only switch on once the ceiling is closed;
- no performance claim, no logo (no official logo file in the repo; brand tokens are
  still placeholders).

**Not allowed:** presenter, voice, lip-sync, AI video (Wan forbidden; Seedance not
needed, 100 % code).
