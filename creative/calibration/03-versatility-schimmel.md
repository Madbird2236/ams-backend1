# Calibration 03 — Versatility: mould in winter (B2C) — study, no credits

## Brief (same for all three)
- **Audience:** B2C: owners and tenants in the Zürich area (private landlords included).
- **Commercial objective:** winter leads for mould remediation (Schimmelsanierung): Begehungen in Nov–Feb, team utilisation.
- **KPI:** site-visit requests (Begehung), cost per lead, request → offer rate in AMS OS.
- **Service:** Schimmel is in AMS's service list. Approved wording: «fachgerecht saniert».
- **Consumer insight:**
  1. Mould peaks in winter. Warm, humid indoor air condenses on cold spots: outer walls, corners, behind furniture.
  2. People notice it late, because it grows where nobody looks.
  3. People feel **shame** («we don't air properly») and fear a dispute with the landlord or a deposit deduction. Shame delays the call.
  4. They don't want to be lectured; they want the problem gone and to know it won't be painted over.
- **Claims guardrails (pre-flight 3):**
  - No «dauerhaft», «garantiert schimmelfrei», «für immer».
  - No health promises («gesund», «Allergien»).
  - «Offerte innert 24 Stunden» allowed (B2C) with «werktags, nach der Besichtigung».
  - Anything about **finding the cause** (moisture measurement, thermal bridges) needs Adriano's confirmation that AMS offers it. Marked ⚠ below.

The test: three **different ideas**, each with its own creative logic, not one idea in three looks.

---

## A — Direct advertising · logic: a behavioural trigger (self-check)
**Idea in one sentence:** The ad gives the reader a 10-second task. If they find something, AMS is the next step.

- **Headline:** «Schauen Sie mal hinter den Schrank.»
- **Sub:** «Im Winter wächst Schimmel dort, wo niemand hinschaut. Wir sanieren fachgerecht.»
- **CTA:** «Begehung anfragen». Small print: «Offerte innert 24 Stunden · werktags, nach der Besichtigung».
- **Visual (19/10 house template):**
  - A tidy, bright bedroom. A wardrobe has been pulled about 30 cm away from the outer wall.
  - In the shadow of the gap, a few grey-green mould spots: small and real, not disgusting.
  - The rest of the room is perfect, so the contrast does the work.
- **Why it converts:** it turns a vague worry into an action today. The self-check is the step before the lead. Anyone who looks and finds mould has a reason to click.
- **Pre-flight:**
  - ✓ No guarantee words.
  - ✓ B2C claim with small print.
  - ✓ Swiss German, no ß.
  - ✓ One brand mark (no kicker).
  - Risk: mould imagery can repel. Keep it small, in shadow, in focus.
- **Scores (discounted):** Surprise 3 · Clarity 5 · Ownability 3 · Premium 4 · Feasibility 5.

## B — Conceptual advertising · logic: visual metaphor + Swiss idiom with a double meaning
**Idea in one sentence:** Mould grows where walls are cold, so the wall gets wool socks.

- **Headline:** «Ihre Wand hat kalte Füsse.»
  - Literal: cold feet. Idiom: «kalte Füsse bekommen» = getting nervous.
  - The wall is cold *and* there is something to worry about.
- **Sub:** «An kalten Stellen kondensiert Feuchtigkeit – dort wächst Schimmel. Wir sanieren fachgerecht.»
- **CTA:** «Begehung anfragen».
- **Visual:**
  - The lower corner of a calm, elegant room, where two outer walls meet the floor.
  - A pair of hand-knitted wool socks (or leg warmers) is pulled over the bottom of the corner, as if the wall were wearing them.
  - Above the socks, two or three discreet mould spots. Morning light.
  - **No people, no tools.**
- **Why it works:**
  - It explains the cause (cold spots) without a lecture, and the humour takes away the shame.
  - It shares the winter world of the snowman ad (wool, warmth, interior), so the campaign builds a family.
- **Pre-flight:**
  - ✓ Not a 1st- or 2nd-level cliché.
  - ✓ Culture: the idiom is used in Swiss German.
  - Clarity risk: the socks alone read as «cute». The mould spots and the sub must carry the meaning; test at feed size.
- **Scores (discounted):** Surprise 4 · Clarity 3.5 · Ownability 4 · Premium 4 · Feasibility 4.

## C — Cinematic storytelling · logic: the antagonist tells the story (Reel 30 s)
**Idea in one sentence:** The mould narrates its winter, confident and a little smug, until AMS arrives.

Why this logic:
- Making the mould the villain moves the blame from the family to the problem, which removes the shame barrier.
- A villain narrator creates tension and humour at once.
- It adds the human emotion missing in calibration 02: we watch a family through the mould's eyes.

| Time | Picture | Voice-over (the mould, calm, dry, Swiss Standard German) | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Black. Slow push into a dark corner behind a curtain; frost on the window outside | «Jeden Winter komme ich zurück.» | Wind, a fridge hum |
| 3–9 s | From the corner's POV: the family's evening. Pasta water steaming, a hot shower, laundry on a rack, the window fogging | «Sie kochen. Sie duschen. Sie trocknen Wäsche. Ich liebe diese Familie.» | Warm home sounds, muffled |
| 9–14 s | The wardrobe against the outer wall; the camera slides into the gap. Grey spots spread in time-lapse as calendar days pass on the wall | «Und den Schrank haben sie direkt an die Aussenwand gestellt. Perfekt.» | A slow, smug cello note |
| 14–18 s | Turn: the mother pulls the wardrobe away; her face; the daughter peeks in | «… Oh.» | Silence, then a doorbell |
| 18–25 s | The AMS team arrives: covers furniture, works with care, calm and clean. The mould's voice gets nervous | «Moment. Was machen die? Die schauen sich ja alles genau an …» ⚠ | Precise, rhythmic work sounds |
| 25–30 s | The finished corner, the wardrobe back with a gap; the daughter opens the window for a few minutes in the cold morning and laughs | (no voice; the villain is gone) | Morning birds |
| End card | «Schimmel kommt jeden Winter wieder. Wir nicht.»* / CTA «Begehung anfragen» | — | Chime |

*End line check:
- «Wir nicht» means AMS doesn't need to come back, which implies a lasting result: **⚠ guarantee risk** under pre-flight 3.
- Safer alternatives: «Schimmel hat einen Grund. Wir nehmen ihn ernst.» or «Fachgerecht saniert. Bevor er sich wohlfühlt.»
- Julia's recommendation: «Fachgerecht saniert. Bevor er sich wohlfühlt.» It keeps the villain's tone without promising permanence.

- **Pre-flight:**
  - Must not become disgusting or horror: no face on the mould, voice only.
  - No health claims.
  - ⚠ VO at 18–25 s implies a diagnosis; keep it only if AMS does cause analysis.
- **Scores (discounted):** Surprise 4 · Clarity 4 · Ownability 4 · Premium 4 · Feasibility 2.5 (Reel production, a family cast, a Swiss voice).

## Ideas I discarded (and why)
- **Direct: «Schimmel? Nicht mit uns.»** Already exists (19/10) and is generic: it could be any company.
- **Conceptual: «Das ist kein Muster.»** Mould dots read as designer wallpaper. Clever, but unreadable at feed size and close to the «finish mistaken for something else» mechanic we already use.
- **Storytelling: the moving-out inspection (deposit drama).** Strong fear, but it belongs to «Auszug? Übergabefertig.» and the March moving date, not to winter mould.

## Which approach for which objective
| Objective | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Leads now (Nov–Feb), lowest cost per lead | **A, direct** | Clearest action; meets people who already worry; best for retargeting and people already searching |
| Stop the scroll, reach, recall | **B, conceptual** | Surprise + humour + education; builds the winter campaign family with the snowman; feeds A via retargeting |
| Trust and premium positioning (no painting over) | **C, storytelling** | Emotion, removes shame, shows the care of the work; highest cost, so only after A/B prove the cost per lead |

**Recommended system for this brief:** B opens (social), A converts (retargeting + direct), C later as the brand piece if the numbers justify a Reel.

## Self-critique
- A is the safest and least original; its surprise comes from the task, not the image.
- B depends on one image working at small size; the main risk is clarity, not originality.
- C is the strongest idea and the most expensive; its end line was my first draft with a guarantee risk, caught by the check.
- Open question for Adriano: does AMS measure moisture or diagnose the cause? It changes B's sub and C's 18–25 s.
