## 3. What I really learned (with proof)
1. **Three distinct logics for one objective.** Shown twice (B2C and B2B): action trigger, metaphor and narrative; documentation, witness and journey. No restyled duplicates.
2. **Claims discipline with nuance.** Service facts are checked against the register. Contractual commitments are separated from advertising expressions («Auf den Takt genau» stays; «dauerhaft» goes).
3. **Motion that carries meaning.** Five object-to-object transformations and one shared beat grid for image, type and sound (Maestro animatic, approved as calibration).
4. **Two reusable storytelling devices:** the villain narrator (removes the client's shame) and the repeated detail across places (proves a standard). Both rated the most interesting by Adriano.
5. **Measuring instead of guessing.** Contrast and reading time are measured. A visual impression proved wrong once (slide 4 contrast), and measurement corrected it.
