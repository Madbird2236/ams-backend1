## Rules
1. Default is the original blue. Switch to a variant only when the blue does not work visually.
2. Choose **position first** (cleanest area that does not compete with headline, subject or CTA), **colour second** (variant with enough contrast there; aim ≥ 4.5:1).
3. No boxes, plates or artificial backgrounds when contrast is sufficient. If no clean area works, report it to Adriano with options; never add a box silently.
4. Never change the symbol, typography or proportions; never invent colours.
5. Mono light needs a clean dark zone. AMS imagery is mostly light, so reserve that zone in the image brief before generation when a campaign needs it.
6. Carousels: logo only on the last slide; single ads: one signature per image.

## Visual format (kept) — house template of 2026-10-19
Reference: the five direct **B2C** ads of 19/10/2026 (brief: `creative/campaigns/2026-10-direkt-b2c/brief.md`) («Grau war gestern.», «Schimmel? Nicht mit uns.», «Vorher. Nachher. AMS.», «Die Kante macht den Meister.», «Auszug? Übergabefertig.»). This template stays valid; do not redesign it.
- Format 4:5 (1024×1280 / 1080×1350), one photographic key image, calm surfaces (walls, rooms, details).
- Thin hairline rules at the top and bottom margin; generous side margins (~85 px at 1024 wide).
- Logo top left: **official website SVG** (decided by Adriano 2026-10-21), colour variant per the rules above. The outlined-triangle / monospace wordmark seen in the 19/10 files is **not** the official logo and must not be used again. The five 19/10 ads are references for the format, not pending production; if one is ever reused, it gets the official logo first.
- Dark gradient over the lower ~45 % carrying all text.
- Kicker in letterspaced monospace, brand blue: «AMS MALERGESCHÄFT · ZÜRICH».
- Headline in bold sans, two lines: line 1 white, line 2 brand blue (the twist or the answer).
- One-line subline in white; services separated by « · » or «—».
- Footer: website in monospace bottom left; CTA pill in brand blue with dark uppercase monospace text bottom right (e.g. «BEGEHUNG ANFRAGEN»).
- Typography applied by the composition tool, never AI-rendered.
Conceptual and storytelling pieces may use other layouts but keep the same kicker/headline/CTA logic and logo rules.
