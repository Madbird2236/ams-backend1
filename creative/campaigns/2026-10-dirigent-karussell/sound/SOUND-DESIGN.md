# «Die Sinfonie» — sound design proposal + demo

## Idea
The music is built from construction-tool sounds. The first seconds sound like an orchestra; after the conductor's two taps, the «orchestra» becomes power tools playing in time. The final chord resolves into a shop-door chime.

## Structure (demo `AMS_sinfonie_demo.mp3`, 23.6 s, 100 BPM)
| Time | Section | Sound source |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | «Orchestra» chord (A major) | Synthesised by Julia (own work) |
| 3.4 s | Wrong entry (detuned squeak) | Synthesised (own) |
| 4.6 s | Two baton taps | Synthesised wood knock (own) |
| 5.6–20 s | Groove builds bar by bar: kick → snare → hi-hat → melody → strings → riser | Kick: drill-motor thump (Makita, PD) + sub sine; snare: Skil saw burst (PD); hi-hat: DeWalt motor noise (PD); **melody: Hitachi drill whine re-pitched to notes** (PD); riser: Milwaukee (PD) |
| 20 s | Final hit | All tools + chord |
| 21.6 s | Shop-door chime | Synthesised bell (own) |

## Limits of the demo
- Julia cannot listen to audio; the demo was checked by waveform and structure only. **Adriano's ear is the quality gate.**
- Drill-whine melody is recognisable but synthetic-sounding; a professional mix (real recorded hits, room tone, stereo) would lift it considerably.
