# Karussell «Der Dirigent» — B2B (Retail-Rollouts, Expansion, GU)

Objective: B2B contract acquisition. Audience: retail brands, expansion managers, general contractors.
Benefit: one partner coordinates every trade, so the store opens on the day. Format 1080×1350, 5 slides.
