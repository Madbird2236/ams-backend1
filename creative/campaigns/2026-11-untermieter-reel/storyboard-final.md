# «Der Untermieter» — definitive storyboard (30 s, 9:16, 1080×1920)

**Premise:** a snowman moves into a family's freshly renovated living room for the winter and won't leave.
**Audience:** B2C — owners and tenants in German-speaking Switzerland. **Objective:** immediate lead capture, winter utilisation (Nov–Feb).
**CTA:** «Jetzt Wintertermin sichern» · «Offerte innert 24 Stunden» (authorised for B2C residential).
**Lead destination:** to be decided by Adriano (WhatsApp Business / form on website / phone). Required before launch.
**Claims check:** no «bis Weihnachten», no price promise, no named trades beyond interior work.

## Continuity bible
- Snowman: the approved key visual (`09b55773`): three balls, coal eyes and smile, carrot nose, chunky grey knitted scarf, grey wool slippers. Same in every shot.
- Room: warm off-white smooth walls, light oak floor, boucle armchair, linen sofa, floor-to-ceiling window right, Swiss village with church spire in snow.
- Family: father (40s, navy jumper), mother (40s, camel cardigan), girl (8), golden retriever. Same clothes throughout.
- Light: warm interior, blue-hour exterior. No logo, no text in the image.
