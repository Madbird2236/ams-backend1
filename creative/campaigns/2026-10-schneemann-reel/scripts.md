# «Im Winter zählt das Innere.» — three 30 s Reel scripts

Audience: B2C homeowners and tenants. Objective: winter interior jobs (Nov–Feb), immediate lead capture. CTA claim authorised: «Offerte innert 24 Stunden».

## A — Intelligent humour: «Der Untermieter»
Premise: a snowman moves into a family's freshly renovated living room for the winter and refuses to leave.
Structure: Setup → Escalation → Payoff. VO: one line (end).
| Time | Shot | Action | Sound | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Front door, interior POV | Doorbell. Father opens: snowman on the doorstep with a tiny suitcase | Blizzard roar through the open door | — |
| 3–8 s | Tracking behind him | He steps in, puts on wool slippers, runs a twig finger along the smooth wall like an inspector, nods | Footsteps, a satisfied «hm» | — |
| 8–15 s | Comedy montage, locked-off wide | Takes the best armchair; reads the newspaper; the dog curls up next to him; the father tries to reclaim the chair, the snowman points at the beautiful room: no chance | Light pizzicato score, newspaper rustle | — |
| 15–22 s | Reaction shot → reveal | Pull back: the whole family sits on the sofa in winter jackets and hats — he turned the thermostat down to 5 °C. He sips iced tea, perfectly happy | Teeth chattering, ice cubes clink | — |
| 22–26 s | MCU snowman by the window | He looks at the snow outside, then at the room, smiles | Music resolves | «Im Winter zählt das Innere.» |
| 26–30 s | End card over the room | — | VO: «Machen Sie Ihr Zuhause so schön, dass niemand mehr gehen will.» | «Innenarbeiten von November bis Februar. Offerte innert 24 Stunden.» |
Benefit: a home so good you never want to leave; winter is the right time. Feasibility: high (one character, one set, approved key visual).

## B — Emotional cinematic: «Das Fenster»
Premise: a snowman a child built watches through the window as the family's room is renewed for the holidays.
Structure: Emotional story → Brand connection. VO: none until the end.
| Time | Shot | Action | Sound | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Wide garden, dusk | Grandfather and child finish a snowman facing the house | Snow crunch, child laughing | — |
| 4–14 s | From the snowman's POV through the window, days passing | Inside, the dull living room is quietly refreshed: furniture covered, then fresh walls, warm light, a tree goes up | Piano motif, muffled through glass | — |
| 14–22 s | Interior, evening | The family gathers, dinner, warmth; the child glances outside at the snowman | Piano swells, cutlery, voices | — |
| 22–27 s | Window CU | The child breathes on the glass and draws a heart facing the snowman | Silence, one note | «Im Winter zählt das Innere.» |
| 27–30 s | End card | — | VO: «Für die Monate, die wir drinnen verbringen.» | «Innenarbeiten bis Weihnachten – Offerte innert 24 Stunden.» |
Risk: «bis Weihnachten» needs capacity confirmation; emotional Christmas ads are crowded, so it is less distinctive.

## C — Unexpected conceptual: «Innen»
Premise: we push into the snowman and discover that inside he is a warm, finished living room.
Structure: Visual metaphor → Revelation → Brand benefit. VO: none.
| Time | Shot | Action | Sound | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Static wide, snowy field at night | A lone snowman, perfectly still | Wind, total cold | — |
| 5–12 s | Slow push-in onto one coal button | The button is a peephole; warm light leaks out | Wind fades, a fire crackles | — |
| 12–22 s | Inside: one continuous move | A beautifully finished living room inside the snowman: smooth walls, lamp light, a family reading | Warm room tone, soft music | — |
| 22–27 s | Pull back out | The snowman's eyes glow like lit windows in the dark field | Wind returns, softened | «Im Winter zählt das Innere.» |
| 27–30 s | End card | — | — | «Innenarbeiten November bis Februar. Offerte innert 24 Stunden.» |
Risk: heavy VFX; the benefit (interior work) is poetic rather than explicit.

**Recommendation: A «Der Untermieter».** It continues the approved key visual, gets a laugh in the first 3 seconds, makes the benefit obvious, and is the cheapest to produce consistently. The thermostat joke is the line people will repeat. B is the stronger feeling but looks like many Christmas ads; C is the most original but the hardest to produce and the least clear.
