## Pre-flight check (before any credit is spent)
1. **Objective → style:** is the style justified by the objective and the KPI?
2. **Idea:** can it be said in one sentence? Would a stranger stop scrolling? Is there a 2nd-level cliché?
2b. **Silent test (an aid, not a law):** cover the headline. Does the image alone show the idea? If the metaphor only lives in the words, the image needs a stronger visual cue (exaggeration, a recognisable object in the wrong place, a familiar gesture). Hierarchy: within 1 s, is the key element (e.g. the mould) the first thing the eye finds? Some ideas work precisely because text and image combine unexpectedly; there, test the pair, not the image alone.
3. **Claims, two kinds (calibrated 2026-10-22):**
   - **Contractual commitment:** a statement a client could hold AMS to, such as a date, a deadline, a duration, permanence of a result, a scope of services, or a number. It needs proof or a softer frame. Examples: «am geplanten Tag», «dauerhaft», «nie wieder», «alle Gewerke», «innert 24 Stunden» (B2B).
   - **Legitimate advertising expression:** figurative, evaluative or tonal language that no reasonable reader takes as a contract. Keep it. Examples: «Auf den Takt genau.», «Eine Handschrift.», «Die Kante macht den Meister.»
   - The promise-word list triggers a **review**, not an automatic deletion. Ask: «Could a client sue or claim a discount on this sentence?» If not, keep the good line. Don't kill strong copy out of excess caution.
   - Service facts are always checked against the claims register (e.g. mould = surface removal and treatment only).
4. **Culture and season:** Swiss details (yellow post boxes, CHF, ss not ß); can AMS actually execute it in this season?
5. **Composition plan:** where do the headline, subject, CTA and logo sit? Is a clean zone reserved for the logo, and which variant?
6. **Brand:** official logo only; no invented colours; no boxes; brand name once per layout (logo *or* kicker).
6a. **Truth in B2B:** communicate real benefits only. Never invent track record, references, numbers or capabilities (e.g. «Filiale 7», «five-city rollout»), even as placeholders in visuals. Confirmed facts only (claims register).
6b. **Legibility:** sublines ≥ 40 px on 1080 wide; contrast measured (≥ 4.5:1), never judged by eye.
6c. **Total duration:** add up every scene **including the end card**, and give each text its reading time (≈ 0.3 s per word + 0.5 s). The stated length must equal the sum.
6d. **Motion finish (permanent, from KT-1/VOX-1 review 2026-10-22):**
   - **First second:** frame 0–1 s must already carry a strong image or word; no near-empty opening.
   - **Dark scenes:** the key object must separate from the background. Measure it; never rely on a dark-on-dark look.
   - **End card:** headline ≥ 64 px, body ≥ 36 px, CTA ≥ 36 px on 1080 wide; contrast ≥ 4.5:1; nothing behind it competing; held for at least its reading time.
   - **Maps and diagrams:** regions must be clearly distinguishable from each other and from the background; labels ≥ 28 px; the final composition keeps the map and the text apart.
6e. **Facts in explainers:** every count and every «alle/allen» is checked. Switzerland has **four** national languages and language regions (German, French, Italian, Romansh). Never say «in allen Sprachregionen» unless all four are shown **and** confirmed. Geographic scope and working hours are claimed only as confirmed in the claims register, and never generalised beyond what was confirmed.
6f. **Audio delivery:** normalise every deliverable with `motion/engine/loudnorm.sh` to −14 LUFS integrated (±1), true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP. Measure before delivery so levels stay consistent between videos.
6i. **Kinetic type on footage (permanent, Adriano 2026-10-22, from KT-UGC-1):**
   - **Legibility beats integration:** spatial type may sit in the scene, but no occluder (stud, person, object) may cut a key word. Place the word in a clear zone of the plane, or move it in front of the occluder. Splitting a word around an obstacle fails.
   - **No long freezes:** a hold longer than ~1.5 s needs life: micro-animation (drift, parallax, breathing scale, light change), a subtle camera move or a change of rhythm. Nothing added only for show.
   - **One reading layer on mobile:** one primary text layer at a time. A secondary layer (e.g. the literal transcript) is optional, smaller, lower contrast and never animates while the primary layer animates. Test at phone size (≈ 360 px wide preview).
6g. **Voice, image and claims (permanent, Adriano 2026-10-22, from UGC re-edit 1). Three checks, run line by line before any publication:**
   - **Voice–image coherence, per line and per moment:** for every spoken line and every subtitle, freeze the frames under it and ask «Does the image say the same thing *right now*?» Checking the whole video is not enough. Failure seen: «Ohne Dämmung drin …» over a shot where the insulation is already being fitted (word says *absent*, image shows *present*).
   - **Technical precision:** performance statements (acoustic, thermal, moisture, durability) say what the measure *does*, never an absolute result. Drywall with insulation *reduces* sound transmission; it does not make a room silent. Failure seen: «… und es wird still.» Safe frame: «deutlich leiser», «dämpft den Schall» or a comparison the image proves.
   - **Visual proof of claims:** every technical feature named (e.g. «doppelt beplankt») must be clearly visible on screen while it is named, or it is cut. Failure seen: two board layers named, one shown.
   - **Sound design and type must not amplify a claim:** a fade to silence or a weighted word under «still» turns an overclaim into a promise. Effects follow the true statement, never the stronger one.
   - A divergence is fixed (re-cut, re-word or re-shoot) before publication. A demo may show it only if its evaluation names it as an open defect.
6h. **Technically better ≠ memorable:** post-production can fix order, continuity, rhythm and sound, but it cannot turn a conventional idea into a memorable one. Judge the idea first (point 2), the finish second.
7. **Cost:** cheapest test first (layout on paper, composition, animatic) before generation.
A piece that fails any point is fixed on paper, not with credits.

**After generation:** check storyboard fidelity (each frame against its function: setting, time of day, state of the space) and execution fidelity (e.g. a drawn object must match the drawing).

**Before writing a new rule:** test it against the approved references. A rule that would forbid an approved piece is wrong; refine it instead.

**Self-scores:** Julia's scores ran about 1 point optimistic (retro test 01). Justify every 5 against an approved reference; only concepts with Surprise ≥ 4 and Clarity ≥ 4 lead a presentation.

## Production route (permanent, Adriano 2026-10-22)
Every project chooses consciously between **real filming, AI generation and hybrid composition**. The technology serves the idea; no project's conclusion becomes a rule for all campaigns. AI video stays an essential competence.

| Question | Points to |
|---|---|
| Does the climax have to *prove* real craft (an edge, a finish, a real site)? | Real footage for that moment |
| Is the idea impossible or too costly to film (metaphor, impossible camera, a winter in October, many locations)? | AI or motion design / 3D |
| Are people the core (performance, humour, trust)? | Real people if available; AI presenters only labelled «KI-generiert», never posing as AMS staff |
| Is it a repeatable series? | The route with the lowest *marginal* cost per episode |
| Full cost, not just credits: team time, preparation, travel, site access, retakes, deadline | Compare all of it |
| Hands, tape, tools, fine physics on screen? | AI is weakest here: test the hardest shot first, smallest draft |

Write the choice and the reason in each brief (one line per question that matters).

## Calibration rule
Julia scores each concept (surprise, clarity, ownability, premium fit, feasibility; 1–5) **before** Adriano sees it. Adriano's verdict is logged in `creative/LEARNINGS.md`. The gap between Julia's score and Adriano's verdict is the measure of judgement; the goal is to shrink it.
Once ads run, market data (CTR, cost per lead, leads → offers in AMS OS) overrides both.
