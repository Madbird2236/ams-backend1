# Motion lab — exercise program (phase 2)

Each exercise is short (5–10 s), trains **one** competency, uses the local engine, and costs no credits. Each one has a success criterion that can be checked, a self-score given *before* Adriano's verdict (to keep calibrating), and a log entry.

The content is always AMS-relevant and claims-safe (confirmed facts only), so that a good exercise can later become a real asset.

## Wave 1 — priority: kinetic typography, Vox language, camera
| # | Exercise | Competency | Content (AMS) | Success criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KT-1 | **Voice-led type** | Kinetic typography | «Schimmel entdeckt? Wir kümmern uns um die betroffenen Oberflächen.» with Piper VO: question big and fast, answer calm, key words weighted | Every word lands with its spoken word (±2 frames); each line on screen ≥ its reading time |
| KT-2 | **Word becomes object** | Kinetic type + morph | «Kante»: the letters straighten into a perfect painted edge, then back into the word | Without any other text, the viewer understands «edge = precision» |
| VOX-1 | **Map of Switzerland** | Vox map language | Wide Switzerland → three language regions tint → five city pins draw → labels «Eröffnung · Ouverture · Apertura» | Info hierarchy: one new fact per beat; labels readable; attribution «© swisstopo» |
| VOX-2 | **Why mould grows in winter** | Vox explainer: chart + collage | A warm room and a cold wall as paper cut-outs, a simple temperature/humidity line with a «condensation» annotation, then AMS's role (removal and surface treatment) | Accurate (no diagnosis claim); understandable with the sound off; ≤ 10 s |
| CAM-1 | **2.5D parallax** | Camera, depth | Layered winter living room (vector layers at 4 depths), slow push-in with depth of field | Depth reads clearly; no layer «slides» unnaturally; motion stays calm |
| CAM-2 | **Crash zoom + whip pan with speed ramp** | Camera, energy | Villain beat «… Perfekt.»: crash zoom on the gap behind the wardrobe, whip pan to the next scene | The cut is invisible inside the blur; the zoom lands on the word «Perfekt.» |

## How each exercise is judged
1. Run the script checker: duration, reading time, VO pace.
2. Pre-flight check (claims, brand, legibility, contrast measured).
3. Self-score on the exercise's own criterion, plus clarity, rhythm and meaning (1–5, honest).
4. Adriano's verdict, logged next to the self-score (calibration of clarity, weakness 2).
5. One lesson per exercise into `creative/LEARNINGS.md`.
