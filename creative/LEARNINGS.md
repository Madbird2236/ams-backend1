## 2026-10-21 — Logo colour and style arsenal
- Don't reinvent the format; evolve one variable at a time. Here the only change was the logo colour.
- Logo: original blue by default; mono dark/light only when contrast fails; position first, colour second; no boxes when contrast suffices.
- Mono light failed on the conductor slide (1.4:1): our light, warm imagery rarely has clean dark zones. Plan the zone in the image brief.
- Keep direct, conceptual and storytelling ads; the campaign objective chooses.
- 2026-10-21: the website SVG is the only official logo. The outlined-triangle wordmark from the 19/10 ads is retired; check every template's logo against the master before reuse.
- 2026-10-21 Calibration 01 (retro test): check catches claims/culture/brand; missed rule-writing and execution fidelity. New: promise words in B2B (camping, conductor), storyboard drift on carousel slide 4, self-scores ~1 point optimistic. Details: `creative/calibration/01-retro-test.md`.
- 2026-10-22 Calibration 02 (motion): transformations carry continuity (baton → pole → key, staves → light lines). Lessons: give every line its reading time; measure contrast of type on brand blue (white fails); synthetic audio and TTS are for timing only.
- 2026-10-22 Calibration 02 **approved as audiovisual calibration** (not final finish). Lessons to keep:
  - **Legibility:** on-screen text needs reading time (≈ 0.3 s per word + 0.5 s); long approved lines must be shortened for motion or the piece lengthened. Measure contrast of every text/background pair (white on brand blue fails).
  - **Rhythm:** one beat grid shared by motion, type and sound; every key event on a beat. Keep this method.
  - **Sound quality:** synthetic sound and TTS are for timing only; final pieces need recorded or licensed sounds and a Swiss voice.
  - **Music → architecture:** the strongest transition was meaning-driven (staves → ceiling lights, score → Bauprogramm). Seek transitions where the object changes meaning, not just shape.
  - **Human emotion:** abstract motion proves structure but lacks feeling; final pieces need human beats (faces, hands, a reaction, a joke).

## 2026-10-22 — UGC re-edit 1 (Trockenbau demo footage, 0 credits)
- Many generation errors are fixable in the edit: re-order shots to the real craft sequence, punch in to hide continuity errors, leave one shot without text as a breathing pause.
- Sound can carry the message: «es wird still» is stronger when the room tone actually falls into silence.
- Limits of post: language of the speech, source resolution and what the image shows stay as generated. Check these before choosing post over a new generation.
- Adriano's verdict (2026-10-22): valid as a post-production demo. Two permanent rules added to the pre-flight: 6g (narration must match the image before any publication) and 6h (technically better ≠ memorable; judge the idea first).
- Adriano's second review (2026-10-22) found two defects my QA missed: (1) «Ohne Dämmung drin» plays over insulation being fitted: I checked subtitles against the *audio*, not against the *image* under them; (2) «und es wird still» overclaims acoustic performance, and my sound design (fade to true silence, weighted «still») made it worse; «doppelt beplankt» was noted but not treated as blocking. Fix: pre-flight 6g now runs voice–image, technical precision and visual proof **line by line**, and effects must follow the true statement. Exercise closed as a post-production demo; kept: continuity fixes, action-led cutting (match on action), sound design that serves meaning.

## 2026-10-22 — KT-UGC-1 (voice-synced kinetic typography on UGC)
- Two layers, two syncs: literal word-by-word in the audio's language (English), translated meaning units timed to the onset of the English unit that carries the meaning. Never fake word-level sync across languages.
- Transcript timestamps drift (first run put «No» 0.34 s early): verify onsets against the audio (voicing analysis) before animating.
- Choosing the passage is part of the type design: playing «no insulation» over the empty cavity fixed the voice–image error before a single letter moved.
- Type in the scene must respect the scene: lay words out so occluders (the stud) fall in the gaps, or the key word becomes unreadable.
- `fromTo` before its start time shows the *from* state: hidden elements (rings) must use `set` at their start, not `fromTo`.
- KT-UGC-1 verdict: approved as technical prototype. New pre-flight 6i: key words never cut by occluders; no hold > ~1.5 s without micro-life; one primary reading layer on mobile. Techniques kept: tracking, spatial depth, type that embodies meaning.

## 2026-10-22 — «Chef, wann?» animatic v1
- A running gag needs one fixed visual signature (same stamp, same place) so it reads with sound off.
- Silence is a beat: it needs a clear trigger (one gesture) and ~0.8–1 s; any overlapping line kills it (v1 draft had «Und jetzt?» still running when the finger rose).
- Open every reveal on the thing revealed (the edge), not on the character.
- Temp TTS proves timing, not comic performance; test humour with real voices before production.
- «Chef, wann?» v2 (Adriano's notes): gestures need silhouette, outline and size to read silent; a climax needs one uninterrupted movement and time without text; a closing line after the climax steals it (move series hooks to the caption). An animatic proves timing, not tactile satisfaction.

## 2026-10-22 — MACRO-1 (paint edge in macro)
- Building a scene ≠ directing a moment: one idea, one move, the change fills the frame.
- Look *along* a surface (grazing camera + raking light) to reveal material; depth of field turns perspective into depth.
- Let the subject cross the frame (camera slower than the subject) instead of pinning it.
- Headless Chromium blocks ES modules over file://: write post-processing passes as classic scripts (own bokeh pass from the depth buffer).
