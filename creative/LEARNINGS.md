## 2026-10-21 — Logo colour and style arsenal
- Don't reinvent the format; evolve one variable at a time. Here the only change was the logo colour.
- Logo: original blue by default; mono dark/light only when contrast fails; position first, colour second; no boxes when contrast suffices.
- Mono light failed on the conductor slide (1.4:1): our light, warm imagery rarely has clean dark zones. Plan the zone in the image brief.
- Keep direct, conceptual and storytelling ads; the campaign objective chooses.
- 2026-10-21: the website SVG is the only official logo. The outlined-triangle wordmark from the 19/10 ads is retired; check every template's logo against the master before reuse.
- 2026-10-21 Calibration 01 (retro test): check catches claims/culture/brand; missed rule-writing and execution fidelity. New: promise words in B2B (camping, conductor), storyboard drift on carousel slide 4, self-scores ~1 point optimistic. Details: `creative/calibration/01-retro-test.md`.
- 2026-10-22 Calibration 02 (motion): transformations carry continuity (baton → pole → key, staves → light lines). Lessons: give every line its reading time; measure contrast of type on brand blue (white fails); synthetic audio and TTS are for timing only.
- 2026-10-22 Calibration 02 **approved as audiovisual calibration** (not final finish). Lessons to keep:
  - **Legibility:** on-screen text needs reading time (≈ 0.3 s per word + 0.5 s); long approved lines must be shortened for motion or the piece lengthened. Measure contrast of every text/background pair (white on brand blue fails).
  - **Rhythm:** one beat grid shared by motion, type and sound; every key event on a beat. Keep this method.
  - **Sound quality:** synthetic sound and TTS are for timing only; final pieces need recorded or licensed sounds and a Swiss voice.
  - **Music → architecture:** the strongest transition was meaning-driven (staves → ceiling lights, score → Bauprogramm). Seek transitions where the object changes meaning, not just shape.
  - **Human emotion:** abstract motion proves structure but lacks feeling; final pieces need human beats (faces, hands, a reaction, a joke).
- 2026-10-22 Calibration 03 (mould ×3) **approved as concept exercise.**
  - A (self-check «Schauen Sie mal hinter den Schrank.»): strong commercial logic; needs clearer hierarchy and a more evident mould discovery.
  - B («Ihre Wand hat kalte Füsse.»): the words beat the picture. Lesson: a visual metaphor must pass the **silent test** (understood without the headline).
  - C (the mould as villain-narrator): the most creative; **kept in the repertoire** as a reusable device (an antagonist narrator removes the client's shame).
  - Precision: never imply a surface treatment ends mould for good when moisture causes remain.
  - Calibrated claims rule: distinguish **contractual commitments** (need proof) from **legitimate advertising expressions** (keep). Excess caution kills good lines.
- 2026-10-22 Service facts confirmed: night/weekend work in operating centres; all Swiss language regions; real rollout photos available (client permission per store). Real proof beats AI images in B2B.

B (night guard) = exercise only: the link witness → service → client benefit is unclear. C (one detail through five cities) = storytelling language **kept in the repertoire**.
