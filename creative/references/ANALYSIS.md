## ref-01 — «Mehr als nur Wände.»
- **What we see:** a child sits on the floor of an unrenovated room (bare plaster, worn parquet), chin in hand, looking up at crayon drawings on the wall: a house, a rocket, a sun, a tree. Warm late sunlight falls through the window as a bright rectangle exactly where the drawings are. A teddy bear and a toy car sit beside the child.
- **Mechanism:** unexpected perspective + emotional metaphor. The wall is not shown as a surface to paint but as a screen for dreams and future life. The light frames the drawings like a projection of what the room *will become*.
- **Hidden benefit communicated:** we don't paint walls, we build the place where your life happens. Renovation = potential, future, home.
- **Composition:** strong diagonal — child (lower left) → light rectangle and drawings (right). Headline sits in the empty upper-left area. Warm, cinematic golden-hour palette; bold white sans-serif with short line breaks. Clear focal point, generous negative space, 9:16 vertical.
- **Slogan & why it works:** «Mehr als nur Wände.» Short, correct Swiss Standard German (no ß issue), states the reframe directly. Weakness: generic — similar lines are used by many painters and builders («Mehr als Farbe», «Mehr als ein Zuhause»).
- **Role:** reference for conceptual creativity, not a template to reproduce.
- **What to reuse (principle):** show the human meaning of the space, not the craft. Light as a storytelling device. One emotional figure, one clear gaze direction. Headline in negative space.
- **What NOT to copy:** children, crayon drawings, dream-on-the-wall metaphor, golden-hour empty room.
- **Risks noted:**
  - *Audience:* private family home (B2C). AMS's strategic targets are retail brands, asset managers, architects, GCs (B2B). For B2B, translate the principle (human meaning of the space) to e.g. store staff, shoppers, the opening day, not to families.
  - *Ownability:* the image alone could belong to other home-related brands; AMS ownership comes from the brand layer, the slogan and campaign context, not from showing the service. Strengthen the slogan rather than adding construction imagery.
  - *AI-generated look:* typical AI-image signs. Fine for social media; check hands, feet and fine details before print or large formats.
