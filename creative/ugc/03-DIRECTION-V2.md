# UGC «Klebeband» — direction v2 (no generation yet)

## Principles (UGC grammar, revised)
1. **Start inside a situation**, not with a presenter: a mistake happening, a surprising detail, a sound, a question someone asks.
2. **The character does the work.** He talks while working; eye contact only where it means something (a reaction, the payoff).
3. **A relationship beats a monologue:** someone films him (an apprentice, a client, a colleague) and can ask a question off camera.
4. **Imperfection is credibility:** a real (not staged) room, dust, a small slip, a sigh, a half-finished sentence.
5. **Explain with a sensory image**, end with a feeling or a visible result, never with a rule.
6. **One generation, several angles** (Seedance 2.5 multi-shot, as in the reference), so the light, room and sound stay one world.
7. **Technically correct, and honest** (manufacturer-aligned; «KI-generiert»).

## Three concepts
**A — «Zu spät» (the mistake happens on camera)**
- Close-up: a hand pulls old tape and the paint film tears in a long strip. A spontaneous «Nei… nei, nei.»
- Wide: the painter sighs, half-laughs, to the person filming: «Zu lange drauf gelassen. Passiert.»
- Next wall, same day: the paint is touch-dry, he pulls slowly at about 45° and a razor line appears. «So – angetrocknet, langsam, schräg.»
- He looks at the line, small nod: «Das ist die Kante.»
- Strength: the failure creates instant curiosity and empathy.

**B — «Die Frage» (question hook, like the reference)**
- Wide, he is on a ladder removing tape: «Warum reisst die Farbe, wenn man das Klebeband abzieht?»
- Close-up: «Weil sie schon eine Haut gebildet hat – die reisst mit.»
- Top-down hands: a slow pull at about 45°.
- Payoff, turning to camera: «Angetrocknet abziehen. Dann bleibt die Kante scharf.»
- Strength: closest to the proven structure.

**C — «Chef, wann?» (mentor moment, filmed by the apprentice)** ← recommended
- The apprentice films on the phone, voice off camera: «Chef, wann ziehe ich das Band ab?»
- The master keeps working, glances back: «Nicht jetzt – die Farbe ist noch nass. Das verschmiert.»
- Cut, a little later: he touches the paint with a knuckle: «Jetzt. Angetrocknet.»
- Top-down hands, a slow pull at about 45°, a crisp line: «Langsam … schräg … fertig.»
- He grins at the apprentice: «Siehst du? Saubere Kante.»
- Strength: a story with two people, spontaneity, the viewer learns like the apprentice. Very UGC, very human.

All three: German, natural; the presenter can speak Swiss-flavoured Standard German. Discreet subtitles, «KI-generiert».

## Character decision (Adriano)
The reference uses **«Adriano dos Santos»**, the owner's own character element.
- **Option 1 — Adriano's twin:** «the boss shows how it's done» is strong for a premium craft business and authentic, because he is the real owner. It still needs the «KI-generiert» label (he did not film it). Risk: the owner's face becomes the face of every AI video, and viewers will judge the brand by it.
- **Option 2 — a fictional painter:** less personal; must never pose as a real employee.
