## Critical evaluation
**Does the idea work?** Yes, structurally. The viewer is made to wait for the satisfying pull, exactly as the colleague is. The repeated stamp makes the gag readable without sound, and the payoff (the line) is also the end card. No service list, no price, no «Qualität» claim: the brand appears only as the signature of a story about patience. That is the difference from conventional construction ads.

**Is the humour timing right?** Partly, and this is the main limit of the test.
- Strongest beat: the silent raised finger. The escalation (beat → no beat → silence) works.
- Temp voices cannot act: Piper reads «Noch nicht.» flat, so the second answer lacks the dry, tired tone that makes it funny. Timing is proven; performance is not.
- B4 (knuckle, 2.3 s) is the slowest part; in a final cut it should lose ~0.4 s, or the suspense turns into drag.
- The final line competes with the reveal for attention. Keep it, but let the line breathe ~0.3 s longer before he speaks.

**Is it different from conventional advertising?** Mostly. The setting is still «painter at work», so the originality is in the structure (withheld gratification, running gag, series hook), not in the image. The concept stays memorable only if the execution is precise: a perfect edge in macro and real comic timing.

**Pre-flight 6g/6i:** «Jetzt.» follows a touch test (touch-dry, manufacturer-aligned); «Langsam. Schräg.» matches the 45° pull on screen; no performance claims; the end line is the approved «Die Kante macht den Meister.». One reading layer, no frozen holds (handheld drift throughout), no occluder over key words.
